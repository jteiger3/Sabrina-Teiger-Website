Sabrina Teiger is a transformational guide who believes that the most significant journey we ever take is the one inward. Her practice is centered on uncovering the barriers people have built around their hearts and supports them in connecting with their innate loving. This connection fosters a way of living that is resilient and authentic. Through her strong co-creative relationship with Spirit, and personal transformative experience, she helps people learn to love and care for themselves in new ways.

About me

Sabrina Teiger is a personal development coach dedicated to helping individuals rediscover the profound love that exists within themselves. Through compassion and clarity, she guides you on a transformative journey of inner connection and empowers you to live an authentic, soulful life. Sabrina has a Masters in Spiritual Psychology with an emphasis in Consciousness, Health and Healing as well as a Masters in Spiritual Science. She has been married over 30 years, has three boys and currently lives in Ojai, CA.
